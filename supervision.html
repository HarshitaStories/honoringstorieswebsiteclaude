Supervision Sessions

Thoughtful, collaborative supervision for practicing therapists and counselors, whether you're early in your career or years into your practice. A space to reflect on your work, your clients, and yourself as a practitioner, and to deepen how you hold the work with care.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I start my supervision journey?

Reach out by email or WhatsApp whenever it feels right, and share a few basic details. We will set up a free 20-minute call first, so you can get a feel for whether this is a space that fits what you are looking for.

What should I prepare before a session?

It helps to jot down what has been on your mind, a case, a feeling, a question you can't quite shake. Join on time, somewhere quiet, with steady internet. We will shape your goals together after that first session.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. The same care around privacy that applies to therapy applies here too, our conversations stay between us, with the one exception of risk of harm, which we would always discuss openly first.

How often will we meet?

Generally every two weeks or so, though we will shape that schedule around what actually supports your practice and pace.

What topics can we cover?

Whatever is alive for you as a practitioner, client cases, ethical grey areas, burnout, imposter syndrome, the everyday logistics of building your practice. If you are unsure whether something belongs here, bring it anyway.

What's the cancellation policy?

You are welcome to reschedule or cancel at no cost up to 3 hours before your session.

Let's begin a conversation

If this feels like a space you'd like to bring your practice into, I'd gently invite you to reach out. A free exploratory call is a soft first step.
